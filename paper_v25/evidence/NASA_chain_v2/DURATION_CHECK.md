# Exploratory mechanism check
Whole-cell confidence grades coincided with bench arms, motivating a post hoc duration check. Event-free recorded steps lasted about 60 s in the skew arms and 300 s in the three uniform arms. Re-run the exact same split, weights, grade cutoffs and rule algorithms with the endpoint changed to recorded cutoff termination by 60 s (Vend<=3.25 and dur_s<=60). Check absence of event-free records shorter than 60 s. This observes termination, not first voltage-threshold crossing. Age, load history, censoring at planned end and arm-specific protocols still differ. This exercise and an arm-only baseline are diagnostic, not new confirmatory tests. The training/tuning design is identical. No held-out safety threshold is optimized.
